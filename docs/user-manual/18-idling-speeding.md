# Idling & Speeding

## Overview

Monitors two wasteful behaviours: prolonged idling (engine running while stationary) and over-speeding. Both burn fuel and wear out machines; this screen shows where and when they happen.

## What you see

**Summary figures.** Counts of idling stretches and over-speed runs in the chosen period.

**Detail list.** Each event with the machine, hub, when it happened, how long (idling) or how fast (speeding), and the limit that was exceeded.

**Limits.** The idling and speeding limits are adjustable so they match your operating rules.


## Investigate idling or speeding

1. Open **Idling & Speeding**.
2. Scan the list for your hub's machines.
3. Note the machine and the time of the event.
4. Discuss with the operator or supervisor and record any corrective action in a work order or incident report.

## Role notes

- Hub Supervisors see their own hub's events only.

> **Tip:** A machine idling for a long stretch may also be a sign the operator has left the engine on while off-site — cross-check the machine's position on the map.
