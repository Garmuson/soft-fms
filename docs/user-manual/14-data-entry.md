# Enter Data

## Overview

The data office screen for typing paper records into the system: fuel chits and maintenance records. Depending on your role, entries may go straight in or wait in the Review Queue for approval.

## What you see

**Tabs.** **Fuel** and **Maintenance** for everyone; the **Projects** tab is shown only to HQ Administrators and Hub Supervisors.

**Fuel tab.** Machine, gallons dispensed, cost, gauge before and after, and notes — the same information as a paper fuel chit.

**Maintenance tab.** Machine, type of work, description, parts used, labour hours, technician name, cost and schedule dates.

**Review state.** If your role's entries need approval, the message says 'sent for review'; otherwise the record is saved immediately.


## Enter a fuel record

1. Open **Enter Data** and stay on the **Fuel** tab.
2. Select the **machine** and enter the **gallons** — 'Choose a machine and enter the gallons' appears if missing.
3. Enter the cost in USD and the gauge readings before and after (or leave them as 'Not recorded' if the chit has none).
4. Add notes if needed.
5. Click **Save**. You will see 'Fuel record saved' or 'Fuel record sent for review'.

## Enter a maintenance record

1. Open **Enter Data** and switch to the **Maintenance** tab.
2. Select the **machine** and describe the work — 'Choose a machine and describe the work' appears if missing.
3. Fill in the type, parts used, labour hours, technician name and cost.
4. Set the scheduled and completed dates.
5. Click **Save**. You will see 'Maintenance record saved' or 'sent for review'.

## Role notes

- Data Entry Staff and Data Entry Supervisors type for their scope; the Data Entry Supervisor covers all hubs.
- The **Projects** tab is reserved for HQ Administrators and Hub Supervisors — data entry roles do not see it.

> **Tip:** If an entry goes to the Review Queue, it is not lost — the reviewer sees it and either approves or rejects it with a reason.
