# Activity Log

## Overview

The audit trail. Every meaningful action — sign-ins, status changes, acknowledgements, record approvals, fuel deliveries — is written here with who did it and when. It cannot be edited or deleted.

## What you see

**Activity table.** Columns: When, User, Action, Record, Reference, Detail. Records are kept permanently, newest first.


## Check who did something

1. Open **Activity Log**.
2. Scan the When and User columns, or scroll to the period you care about.
3. Read the Action and Detail columns to see exactly what changed.

## Role notes

- Data Entry Staff and Hub Supervisors do not have this screen; their actions are still recorded.

> **Note:** The log is append-only by design: entries cannot be changed or removed, so it can be trusted in audits.
