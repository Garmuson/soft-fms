# Hub Dashboard & My Machines

## Overview

The Hub Supervisor's home screen for their own hub. It shows machine status, fuel, alerts and today's work at a glance, and lets the supervisor manage machine status.

## What you see

**Hub Dashboard.** Headline figures for the hub (machines, active, in maintenance, open alerts, fuel on hand), the **Hub Fuel Depot** panel with tank gauge and drawdown chart, the hub map, recent alerts and the hub's active projects.

**My Machines.** The hub's machine list with status badges and fuel gauges. Open a machine to see its recent fuel entries and alerts and to change its status.


## Change a machine's status

1. Open **My Machines**.
2. Find the machine and click it to open its details.
3. Choose the new status (Active, Idle, Maintenance, Alert, Offline).
4. The message '<unit ID> marked <status>' confirms, and the change is recorded in the Activity Log.

## Check the hub's fuel position

1. Open **Hub Dashboard**.
2. Read the **Hub Fuel Depot** gauge: current stock, capacity and status (Healthy, Reorder Needed, Critical Low).
3. Check 'days to empty' based on the recent daily burn.
4. If the status is Reorder Needed or Critical Low, record the next delivery (see the Hubs chapter) or arrange one with HQ.

## Role notes

- Everything on these two screens covers your own hub only — other hubs' data is not shown and cannot be reached.

> **Warning:** A machine marked **Maintenance** stops counting as available in reports. Use **Offline** only when the machine is not reporting.
