# Offline Working

## Overview

The field app is designed for areas with weak mobile signal. When the connection drops, entries you make on the device are held safely on the device and sent to HQ when the signal returns.

## What you see

**Connection banner.** An amber bar at the top reads 'Connection lost — showing cached data. Reconnecting…'. When the link returns, a green 'Reconnected' bar appears.

**Pending Sync panel.** On the field screens, a panel lists entries still waiting to be sent, with a **Send now** button and a count.


## Work through a signal drop

1. Keep working — save your fuel entries, inspections and incident reports as normal.
2. The entries wait in the **Pending Sync** panel on the device.
3. When the signal returns, tap **Send now** (or wait — they send automatically).
4. The message 'N held entries sent to HQ' confirms. If there is still no connection you see 'Still no connection — entries are safe on this device'.

> **Warning:** Do not clear your browser data or uninstall the browser while entries are pending — held entries live on the device until they are sent.
