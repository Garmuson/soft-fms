# Inspections

## Overview

The 12-point safety checklist completed before and after operating a machine (and daily). Every item is marked Pass, Fail or N/A; failures require a note describing the problem.

## What you see

**Inspection form.** Choose the machine and the inspection type (Pre-deployment, Post-deployment, Daily), then work through the 12 checklist items.

**On phones** the form shows one step per screen with a 'Step 1 of 14' progress bar and big Back / Next buttons near the thumb. The final step holds the **Submit Inspection** button.

**Failure notes.** Choosing **Fail** on any item opens a box to describe the problem — 'Add notes for every failed item' appears if any failure has no note.


## Submit an inspection

1. Open **Inspection**.
2. Select the **machine** you inspected — 'Select the machine you inspected' appears if empty.
3. Choose the inspection type: **Pre-deployment**, **Post-deployment** or **Daily**.
4. For each of the 12 items, tap **Pass**, **Fail** or **N/A**.
5. For every item marked **Fail**, describe the problem in the note box.
6. Tap **Submit Inspection** on the last step.
7. The inspection is saved with your name and the time; failures raise alerts for the hub.

## Role notes

- You can only inspect machines in your own hub.

> **Warning:** Do not operate a machine with an unresolved Fail on a safety item — report it to your supervisor immediately.
