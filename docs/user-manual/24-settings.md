# Settings

## Overview

Your own profile details, and the system's operational settings. Only HQ Administrators can open this screen.

## What you see

**Profile.** Your **Name** and **Email** as they appear across the system.

**Role.** Your assigned role (set on the Users page, shown here for reference).


## Update your profile

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Change your **Name** if needed.
3. Save — the change appears in the top bar immediately.

## Role notes

- If your email must change, it is safer to ask support — it is also your sign-in identity.
