# Incidents

## Overview

Report a breakdown, accident, damage or tampering from the field, with the location captured automatically where possible.

## What you see

**Incident form.** Machine, incident type, severity, a description of what happened, and an optional GPS location.

**On phones** the form is split into steps with a progress bar and large buttons.


## Report an incident

1. Open **Incident**.
2. Select the **machine** — 'Select a machine and describe what happened' appears if the machine or description is missing.
3. Choose the **Incident type** (e.g. breakdown, accident, tampering).
4. Choose the **Severity**.
5. Describe what happened in the text box — include anything a reader needs to understand the situation.
6. Optionally tap **Capture location** ('Location captured'), or continue without it ('Location is not available').
7. Tap **Submit**.

## Role notes

- Report incidents for your own hub's machines.

> **Warning:** For accidents involving injury, make the site safe and inform your supervisor by phone first; use the app to record the details.
