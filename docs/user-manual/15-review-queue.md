# Review Queue

## Overview

The approval step for records submitted by data entry: fuel entries, maintenance records, driver records and assignments. Nothing enters the official ledger until it is approved here.

## What you see

**Queue table.** Pending records with their type, hub, who entered them and when. Filter by hub.

**Decision buttons.** **Approve** accepts the record into the ledger; **Reject** marks it rejected with a reason.


## Review a record

1. Open **Review Queue**.
2. Open the record and read it against the source document (paper chit or logsheet).
3. Click **Approve** — the message 'Record approved' confirms — or **Reject** and give the reason ('Record rejected').
4. The decision, your name and the time are recorded in the Activity Log.

## Role notes

- HQ Administrators can review every hub; the Data Entry Supervisor reviews nationally as well.

> **Warning:** Approving a record makes it part of the official ledger — check the figures carefully before approving.
